Gesture trainer

On the Quest, with your hands (put the controllers down). Each gesture is asked for 5 times, right hand only: a count of 3, then do it while the bar runs. Shoo comes in two steps (pick the cube, then throw it, or cancel). Your hands are recorded with each take labelled, for training a gesture model later.

Takes on the cube or the button wait until your hand is there, then GO. Between takes: Redo (that take was bad), Pause, Skip, on the strip low on your left: poke them, or aim the beam and pinch.

Train records labelled takes for a gesture model. Test lets you try each gesture 5 times and shows whether today's detectors catch it, how fast, and why not; a "nothing" round counts false alarms. Switch any time with Mode.
